## About Me

**Senior biostatistician** at [Alsinova (Astek)](https://www.alsinova.com/) and, separately, a **freelance** developer specialising in the Quarto and R ecosystems.
I combine expertise in genetics and multi-omics analysis with open-source development.

> [!NOTE]
> **Available as a freelance for short-term and extended engagements.**
> Quarto extensions and multi-format pipelines (HTML, Typst/PDF, Reveal.js), R package development, `shiny` applications, reproducible workflows with Dev Containers and continuous integration, and training.
> Biostatistics, clinical, and data-analysis work goes through Alsinova rather than my freelance offer.

**50 peer-reviewed papers** in top-tier journals, including **Nature**, **Nature Genetics**, and **Diabetes**.
Eight of those place me as first, co-first, last, or co-last author.

## Open Source

- **Creator** of the [Quarto Extensions Directory](https://m.canouil.dev/quarto-extensions/) and [Awesome Quarto](https://github.com/mcanouil/awesome-quarto).
- **Developer** of the [Quarto Wizard](https://github.com/mcanouil/quarto-wizard) VS Code extension, 30+ Quarto extensions, and [Gribouille](https://github.com/mcanouil/gribouille), a grammar of graphics for Typst.
- **Creator** of R packages: [NACHO](https://github.com/mcanouil/NACHO), [insane](https://github.com/mcanouil/insane), [MiSTr](https://github.com/mcanouil/MiSTr), [snpEnrichment](https://github.com/mcanouil/snpEnrichment), and [ggpacman](https://github.com/mcanouil/ggpacman).
